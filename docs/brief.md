# Homezee - Project Brief

## Original Brief
App to save high brokerage and high daily costs using products. Easily find local services like rental property, food, doctors, shops, transport. Local servants like bai, doodh wala, sabji wala, laundry wala, etc. Helps to find city specialities. Basically it helps so we never feel unknown in a new area, and makes daily life easier.

## Understanding
A hyperlocal "neighbourhood guide" app for people new to a city or area, with four parts:
1. **Save brokerage and daily costs:** owner-direct rentals, and fair-price visibility on everyday products.
2. **Local services:** rental property, food, doctors, shops, transport.
3. **Daily helpers:** bai, doodh wala, sabji wala, laundry wala and similar, usually found only by word of mouth.
4. **City specialities:** what each city or locality is known for.
