# Homezee - Solution Statement

## The Problem
Moving to a new city or neighbourhood makes daily life expensive and stressful:
- **High brokerage and hidden costs:** finding a rental usually means paying a broker a month's rent or more. Everyday products often cost more because newcomers don't know fair local prices.
- **No trusted local network:** newcomers don't know which doctor, shop, tiffin service or transport option is good and nearby.
- **Daily helpers are hard to find:** the bai, doodh wala, sabji wala and laundry wala are found only by word of mouth.
- **Feeling like a stranger:** without local knowledge, people miss what makes their new city special.

## The Solution
A hyperlocal app that acts as a **trusted neighbour in your pocket**. Anyone entering a new area can find what they need in minutes, at fair prices and without a middleman.

**Core capabilities**
1. **Save brokerage:** owner-direct rental listings, so tenants skip broker fees.
2. **Save daily costs:** local price visibility and deals on everyday products.
3. **Local services in one place:** rental property, food, doctors, shops and transport, filterable by area, with one-tap call or WhatsApp.
4. **Daily helper directory:** verified, rated bai, milk, vegetable and laundry providers, with rates, timings and service areas.
5. **City specialities:** a guide to what each city or locality is known for.

## Target Users
- Students and working professionals relocating to a new city
- Families shifting neighbourhoods
- Tenants tired of paying brokerage
- Local service providers who want steady customers without an agent

## Value Delivered
| For newcomers | For local providers |
|---|---|
| Lower housing and daily costs | Direct customers with no middleman |
| Settle in days, not months | Visibility in their own area |
| Trusted, rated options | Steady, repeat daily business |
| Never feel like a stranger | Digital presence at zero cost |

## Vision
"Wherever you move, you should feel local from day one."
